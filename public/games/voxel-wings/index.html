How to Play

Move: Use WASD / Arrow keys or drag on mobile screen.
Fire: Hold Space or touch-drag screen to auto-fire.
Special: Press Shift or tap the shield/badge to trigger special weapon when ready.
Objective: Fly through checkpoints, shoot enemy fighters/bosses, collect coins, and buy stronger jets in the Hangar!
